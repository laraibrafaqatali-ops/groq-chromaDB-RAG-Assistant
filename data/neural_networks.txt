Deep learning is a subset of machine learning based on artificial neural networks that mimic the structure and processing methods of the human brain.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are specialized deep learning architectures designed primarily for processing visual imagery, object detection, and spatial feature extraction.

Transfer learning enables developers to take a pre-trained model on a large dataset and fine-tune it on a specific target domain with much less computational cost.

Activation functions like ReLU and Sigmoid introduce non-linearity into neural networks, allowing them to learn complex patterns and decision boundaries in high-dimensional data.

Overfitting occurs when a model learns noise and details in the training data to the extent that it negatively impacts performance on new unseen evaluation data.
