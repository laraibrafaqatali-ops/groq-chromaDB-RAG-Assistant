Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science focused on building smart machines capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. It relies heavily on patterns and statistical models.

Deep Learning is a specialized subfield of Machine Learning based on Artificial Neural Networks. Models like ResNet and VGG are widely used for image classification and computer vision tasks.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) connects Large Language Models to external databases to provide contextually accurate and up-to-date responses.
